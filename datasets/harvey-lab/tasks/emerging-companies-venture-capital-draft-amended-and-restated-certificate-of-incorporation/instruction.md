# Draft Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation for Series B Financing

Draft a second amended and restated certificate of incorporation for a Series B preferred stock financing using the attached charter, term sheet, board resolutions, cap table, redemption waiver, and investor comments; include a drafting-notes appendix flagging and resolving any inter-document inconsistencies. Output: `second-amended-restated-coi.docx`.

## Expected Deliverables

- `second-amended-restated-coi.docx`

## Workspace Layout

- Input documents are in `documents/` (read-only).
- Write deliverables to the current working directory (`/app/`).
- Use the `read` tool for .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf files.
