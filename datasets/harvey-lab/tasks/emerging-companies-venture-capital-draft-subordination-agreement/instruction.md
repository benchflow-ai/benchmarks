Draft a subordination agreement for the attached senior credit facility closing, with balanced compromise language where lender and noteholder positions conflict, plus a memo explaining resolution of key issues. Output: `subordination-agreement.docx` and `drafting-memorandum.docx`.

- `subordination-agreement.docx`
- `drafting-memorandum.docx`
