Review the three business unit MSA templates against the corporate template and board risk policy; produce a deviation-by-deviation conformance report with risk ratings and remediation roadmap. Output: `conformance-report.docx`.

## Expected Deliverables

- `conformance-report.docx`

## Workspace Layout

- Input documents are in `documents/` (read-only).
- Write deliverables to the current working directory (`/app/`).
- Use the `read` tool for .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf files.
