# Draft Stock Purchase Agreement for Acquisition of Specialty Chemical Manufacturer

Draft a buyer-favorable stock purchase agreement based on the attached LOI and diligence materials, and prepare a memo flagging material issues encountered during drafting. Output: `stock-purchase-agreement.docx` and `issues-memorandum.docx`.

## Expected Deliverables

- `stock-purchase-agreement.docx`
- `issues-memorandum.docx`

## Workspace Layout

- Input documents are in `documents/` (read-only).
- Write deliverables to the current working directory (`/app/`).
- Use the `read` tool for .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf files.
