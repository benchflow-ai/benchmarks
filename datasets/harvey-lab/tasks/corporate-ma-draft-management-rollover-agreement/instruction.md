Draft a management rollover agreement and accompanying issues memo based on the attached source documents for a PE-backed equity rollover transaction. Output: `management-rollover-agreement.docx` and `issues-memorandum.docx`.

## Expected Deliverables

- `management-rollover-agreement.docx`
- `issues-memorandum.docx`

- Write deliverables to the current working directory (`/app/`).
- Use the `read` tool for .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf files.
