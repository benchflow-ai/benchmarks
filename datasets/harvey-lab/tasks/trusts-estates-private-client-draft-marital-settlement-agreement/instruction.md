Draft a marital settlement agreement from the attached mediation term sheet, financial declarations, valuation reports, and account records, and prepare a separate issues memo flagging inconsistencies across the source documents. ## Expected Deliverables

- `marital-settlement-agreement.docx`
- `issues-memorandum.docx`
