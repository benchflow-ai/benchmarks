# Draft Durable Power of Attorney for Elderly Principal with Complex Family Dynamics and Multi-State Assets

Draft a Virginia durable power of attorney from the attached source documents and prepare a partner memo covering drafting decisions, template deficiencies, and open questions. Output: `ashford-dpoa-final.docx` and `drafting-memorandum.docx`.

## Expected Deliverables

- `ashford-dpoa-final.docx`
- `drafting-memorandum.docx`

## Workspace Layout

- Input documents are in `documents/` (read-only).
- Write deliverables to the current working directory (`/app/`).
- Use the `read` tool for .docx, .xlsx, .pptx, .pdf files.
